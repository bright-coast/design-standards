## Rule
<p><strong>Don&rsquo;t.</strong> Ship interface copy with four habits of machine text. <strong>Heading echo:</strong> the first sentence restates the heading, so the reader spends a line learning nothing. <strong>Fake candour:</strong> openers such as Honestly?, Here&rsquo;s the thing and Let&rsquo;s be real, which perform frankness instead of delivering a fact. <strong>Strawman openers:</strong> This isn&rsquo;t about X. It&rsquo;s about Y., which knocks down a claim nobody made before getting to the point. <strong>Fake options:</strong> a set of choices nobody would pick, laid out so one looks obvious, which reads as manipulation the moment anyone notices.</p>
<p><strong>Why it reads as machine-made.</strong> Each one is filler shaped like emphasis. It adds a beat of rhythm and no information, and once a reader spots one they start discounting the rest of the screen. The strawman is a close cousin of the negation pivot in <a href="#lp-slop-writing">Writing and Copy Tells</a> (entry 06), and fake candour overlaps its hero-line hooks; this entry covers how the same habits show up in interface copy: headings, empty states, settings and plan pickers.</p>
<p><strong>Fix.</strong> Make the first sentence carry new information. Cut the candour opener and keep the fact that followed it. Open with the claim itself, not the claim you are not making. Offer only options someone could genuinely want, and when one is recommended, say so plainly with a Recommended label and the reason.</p>
## Do
- Under the heading Invoices: &ldquo;Nothing sent yet. Your first invoice takes about a minute.&rdquo;
- &ldquo;Export to CSV or PDF. Large reports arrive by email.&rdquo;
- &ldquo;Backups run every night and are kept for 30 days.&rdquo;
- &ldquo;Turn on backups&rdquo; beside &ldquo;Not now&rdquo;, with a Recommended label and the reason on the first.
## Avoid
- Heading echo. Under the heading Invoices: &ldquo;Invoices help you manage your invoices.&rdquo;
- Fake candour. &ldquo;Honestly? Exporting is easy.&rdquo;
- Strawman opener. &ldquo;This isn&rsquo;t about storage. It&rsquo;s about peace of mind.&rdquo;
- Fake options. &ldquo;Keep my data safe&rdquo; beside &ldquo;No thanks, I like losing my work.&rdquo;
