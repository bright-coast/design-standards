## Rule
<p><strong>Don&rsquo;t.</strong> Ship a screen, a page or a piece of copy that trips any of these lines without having decided it on purpose. This is the whole category as one line per tell, grouped by family, for a fast review pass. The fix for each line is in the entry for its family above. Two lines are marked unverified, where the original claim has not been checked.</p>
## Do
- <span>Add every new tell here as one line the moment it is spotted, in the entry for its family.</span>
## Avoid
- <span><strong>Dashboard.</strong> A gradient header that encodes nothing.</span>
- <span><strong>Dashboard.</strong> Four accent colours on four stat tiles.</span>
- <span><strong>Dashboard.</strong> Every stat at the same size and weight.</span>
- <span><strong>Dashboard.</strong> A drop shadow on every card.</span>
- <span><strong>Dashboard.</strong> A templated greeting and a bare delta (&ldquo;Welcome back, Jordan&rdquo;, &ldquo;+12.5% from last month&rdquo;).</span>
- <span><strong>Landing page.</strong> An adjective-plus-noun headline (&ldquo;Supercharge your workflow with AI&rdquo;).</span>
- <span><strong>Landing page.</strong> An ambient glow behind the hero.</span>
- <span><strong>Landing page.</strong> Two equal-weight calls to action, both left-aligned.</span>
- <span><strong>Landing page.</strong> A trust number nobody can check (&ldquo;Trusted by 10,000+ teams&rdquo;).</span>
- <span><strong>Landing page.</strong> Icon-plus-adjective feature cards.</span>
- <span><strong>Typography.</strong> One line height on everything (unverified).</span>
- <span><strong>Chat UI.</strong> Messages from one sender not grouped.</span>
- <span><strong>Chat UI.</strong> A timestamp on every bubble.</span>
- <span><strong>Chat UI.</strong> A spinner while a sent message waits for the server.</span>
- <span><strong>Chat UI.</strong> A list that jumps to the bottom on every new message.</span>
- <span><strong>Chat UI.</strong> A typing indicator away from the thread.</span>
- <span><strong>Chat UI.</strong> A composer that grows until it pushes the thread out of view.</span>
- <span><strong>Decorative motion.</strong> Choreography after the job is done (the OTP orbit flourish).</span>
- <span><strong>Decorative motion.</strong> A glowing visual that carries no information.</span>
- <span><strong>Writing.</strong> The words genuinely, honestly and straightforward (unverified source).</span>
- <span><strong>Writing.</strong> The negation pivot (&ldquo;not X, not Y, but Z&rdquo;).</span>
- <span><strong>Writing.</strong> Hero-line openers and hooks (&ldquo;X is broken.&rdquo;, &ldquo;Here&rsquo;s the thing.&rdquo;).</span>
- <span><strong>Writing.</strong> Rule-of-three beats, parallel negation lists and one sentence per line.</span>
- <span><strong>Writing.</strong> A first sentence that restates the heading (heading echo).</span>
- <span><strong>Writing.</strong> Fake candour openers (&ldquo;Honestly?&rdquo;, &ldquo;Here&rsquo;s the thing&rdquo;, &ldquo;Let&rsquo;s be real&rdquo;).</span>
- <span><strong>Writing.</strong> Strawman openers (&ldquo;This isn&rsquo;t about X. It&rsquo;s about Y.&rdquo;).</span>
- <span><strong>Writing.</strong> Fake options nobody would pick, laid out so one looks obvious.</span>
- <span><strong>Pricing.</strong> Three identical pricing tiers.</span>
- <span><strong>Pricing.</strong> A badge on every pricing card.</span>
- <span><strong>Pricing.</strong> Twelve repeated check marks per tier.</span>
- <span><strong>Pricing.</strong> A gradient button on every plan.</span>
- <span><strong>Pricing.</strong> A bare percent chip as the only statement of the saving.</span>
- <span><strong>Cards.</strong> A card around everything; a form section is not a card (fix in Card Treatment System, Visual entry 28).</span>
## Extra: caveat-box
<div class="caveat-box"><b>A review aid, not a detector.</b> A hit means a default nobody decided, not proof that a machine made it.</div>
